Refund Policy

Last updated: October 2026

⛔ Important: All sales of digital PDF products are final and non-refundable. Please read this policy carefully before making a purchase.

Why We Cannot Offer Refunds

Our products are delivered as downloadable PDF files. Unlike physical goods, a digital file cannot be "returned" — once a PDF has been downloaded or delivered to your email, we have no way to retract or revoke access to the file.

Because of this fundamental nature of digital products, we are unable to offer refunds, exchanges, or credits once a purchase has been completed and the product has been delivered.

🛈 Before You Buy: We strongly encourage you to review all available information before purchasing. Our product pages include detailed descriptions, book preview carousels showing real pages from the book, feature breakdowns, and FAQ sections — everything you need to make an informed decision.

All Sales Are Final

By completing a purchase on Big Protein, you acknowledge and agree that:

  • You are purchasing a digital product (PDF file)
  • The product will be delivered immediately via download link and/or email
  • Once delivered, the product cannot be returned, undelivered, or retracted
  • You have reviewed the product information and are making an informed purchase decision
  • No refunds, credits, or exchanges will be issued after delivery

What We Recommend Before Purchasing

To make sure this product is right for you:

  • Follow our How to Order Guide to preview checkout screens, currency selection, and coupon application
  • Browse the preview carousel on our homepage to see real pages from the book
  • Read the "What's Inside" section for a complete feature breakdown
  • Check the FAQ for answers to common questions regarding delivery and format
  • Contact us with any questions before you buy — we're happy to help

We want you to be 100% confident in your purchase before you click "Buy." If you're unsure, please reach out first.

Exceptions

While all sales are final, we will do our best to help in the following situations:

  • Wrong email address: If you entered the wrong email and didn't receive your download link, contact us and we'll resend it to the correct address (with verification).
  • Technical issues: If you're experiencing a technical problem downloading or opening the PDF, contact us and we'll help troubleshoot or provide an alternative download method.
  • Duplicate charges: If you were accidentally charged twice for the same product, contact us immediately and we will refund the duplicate charge.
  • Unauthorized purchases: If your payment method was used without your authorization, please contact your bank or payment provider to dispute the charge, and notify us so we can investigate.

Promotional & Free Products

Products received as part of a promotional offer (such as the "Sip Smart, Spend Less" ebook received for free using promo code FREEDRINK) have no cash value and are not eligible for refund, exchange, or credit of any kind.

Chargebacks

If you initiate a chargeback or payment dispute after receiving the product, we reserve the right to:

  • Contest the chargeback with evidence of product delivery
  • Suspend or terminate your access to any future purchases
  • Pursue recovery of costs associated with fraudulent chargebacks

We encourage you to contact us directly before initiating a chargeback so we can resolve any concerns.

Questions Before You Buy?

We want you to love what you buy. If you have any doubts or questions about our products, please reach out to us before purchasing. We're happy to answer anything — what's in the book, who it's for, what equipment you need, or anything else.

Summary: Purchase only when you are sure. Once the PDF is delivered, we cannot take it back, and no refund will be issued. Please review all product information and contact us with any questions before buying.
